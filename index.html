Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Metal Munafa ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from users.

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Portfolio data (gold/silver entries)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

2. How We Use Information

Any stored data is used only to:

  • Display your portfolio and calculations
  • Improve user experience
  • Provide app functionality

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party APIs to fetch:

  • Currency exchange rates

These services may process requests to provide data but do not receive personal user data from us.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining device security.

6. Export & Backup

The app allows users to export their data (CSV or JSON). This is done only when the user explicitly chooses to do so.
